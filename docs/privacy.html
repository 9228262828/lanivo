LANIVO PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Summary: LANIVO is designed as an offline-first lane-switch arcade game. The current core version does not require an account, Firebase, a remote backend, cloud sync, ads, or behavioral analytics.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LANIVO, package com.lanivo.laneswitch, handles information when you use the mobile game.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to the current core version of LANIVO and these informational pages. If future versions materially change data handling, this Policy should be updated before release.

3. Game Data

LANIVO stores limited gameplay information locally, such as best scores, games played, collected coins, best combo, total distance, and game settings.

4. Endless Mode Data

The app may store your best Endless Mode score locally on your device.

5. 60 Sec Rush Data

The app may store your best 60 Sec Rush score locally on your device.

6. Perfect Run Data

The app may store your best Perfect Run score locally on your device.

7. Games Played

The app may keep a local count of completed game sessions.

8. Coins

The app may store the total number of coins collected across game sessions.

9. Best Combo

The app may store your highest combo achieved during gameplay.

10. Distance

The app may keep a local running total of distance-related gameplay progress used for statistics.

11. Dark Mode Preference

Your dark-mode preference may be stored locally so LANIVO can remember your selected appearance.

12. Haptic Feedback Preference

Your haptic-feedback preference may be stored locally so LANIVO can remember whether vibration feedback is enabled.

13. Local Storage

The current core version stores the gameplay information described above locally using application storage such as SharedPreferences.

14. No Account Required

You do not need to create a LANIVO account, username, profile, or password to play the current core version.

15. No Firebase

The current core version is not designed to use Firebase for authentication, analytics, cloud databases, or remote configuration.

16. No Remote Backend

The current core version does not send your gameplay progress to a LANIVO-operated remote backend.

17. No Cloud Sync

The current core version does not provide LANIVO-operated cloud synchronization of scores or preferences between devices.

18. No Advertising

The current core version does not contain LANIVO-operated advertising.

19. No Behavioral Analytics

The current core version is not designed to collect behavioral analytics for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

20. No Cross-App Tracking

LANIVO is not designed to track your activity across unrelated third-party apps or websites.

21. No Sale of Personal Information

LANIVO does not intentionally sell or rent the locally stored gameplay information described in this Policy.

22. Permissions

The described core features do not require location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, calendar, photos, media, or payment permissions.

23. Location

The current core version does not require precise or approximate location information.

24. Camera and Photos

The current core version does not require camera access or photo-library access.

25. Microphone

The current core version does not require microphone access, voice recording, or audio capture.

26. Contacts

The current core version does not require access to your device contacts.

27. Files and Media

The current core version does not require access to your personal files, documents, images, or media library for normal gameplay.

28. Payments

The current core version does not require payment-card details, banking credentials, or payment account information.

29. Haptic Feedback

If haptic feedback is enabled, LANIVO may use the device's built-in vibration or haptic capability to provide touch feedback when switching lanes.

30. Timers and Gameplay Logic

LANIVO uses local timers and game-state logic to control object movement, spawn timing, game duration, scores, combos, distance, obstacle progression, and result screens. This processing occurs on the device.

31. Coins and Obstacles

Coin collection and obstacle interactions are processed locally and are used only to calculate score, combo, run results, and locally stored statistics.

32. Difficulty Progression

LANIVO may gradually adjust speed and spawn timing based on local gameplay progress. This does not require network processing.

33. Statistics

Statistics such as games played, total coins, total distance, best combo, and best scores are calculated from locally stored gameplay data.

34. Data Retention

Locally stored scores and preferences generally remain on your device until you reset progress, clear application storage, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes the data.

35. Reset Progress

LANIVO includes a reset option that deletes locally stored scores and gameplay statistics while leaving app functionality available.

36. Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling LANIVO may remove locally stored gameplay information, subject to operating-system or device backup and restore behavior.

37. Device Backups

Your operating system or device provider may back up application data according to your device settings. Such backups are not controlled by LANIVO's core game logic.

38. Data Recovery

Because the current core version does not include LANIVO-operated cloud backup, we may not be able to recover scores or preferences after deletion, device loss, app removal, or storage failure.

39. Device Security

You are responsible for protecting access to your device using available security features such as a passcode or biometric lock.

40. Children

LANIVO is a casual arcade game intended for a general audience. It is not designed to collect personal information from children through accounts, profiles, forms, or messaging features in the current core version.

41. Third-Party Platforms

App stores and device platforms may independently process technical, account, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies. Those practices are separate from LANIVO's local gameplay data.

42. Google Play

If you download LANIVO through Google Play, Google may process app-installation, account, device, purchase, security, or diagnostic information under Google's own terms and privacy practices.

43. GitHub Pages

These informational pages may be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-server information according to its own policies. These pages do not require you to submit LANIVO gameplay data.

44. Support Communications

If you contact us at adampainting9@gmail.com, your email provider and our email provider will process the information you voluntarily include. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, secret keys, or other highly sensitive information.

45. Legal Requirements

If legally required, information voluntarily provided through support communications may be disclosed to comply with applicable law or valid legal process. The current core app does not maintain a LANIVO-operated cloud database of your gameplay progress.

46. International Use

You may use LANIVO in different countries. Local gameplay data remains on your device in the current core version, while app stores, email providers, and website hosts may process their own data in other jurisdictions.

47. Future Features

If future releases add accounts, cloud sync, multiplayer, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, purchases, leaderboards, online services, or additional permissions, this Policy should be updated before release.

48. Your Choices

You can choose whether to enable haptic feedback, change appearance settings, reset progress, clear app storage, uninstall the app, or stop using LANIVO.

49. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when LANIVO changes or when legal, technical, or app-store requirements change.

50. Contact

For privacy questions about LANIVO, contact adampainting9@gmail.com.
